<h1 align="center">🚗 Vehiclery</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Build your own vehicles, block by block. Frame it, power it, wheel it, drive it.</strong></p>

<br>

---

<br>

<h2>✨ Overview</h2>

<p>Vehiclery adds fully customizable, buildable vehicles to Minecraft (26.2 / NeoForge). Craft frames, engines, wheels and attachments, assemble them on the Automobile Assembler, and drive off — no rails, no pre-made cars, just parts you choose.</p>

<p>The mod is currently in early development (<code>0.0.0-beta.1</code>) — the initial port from Automobility to Minecraft 26.2 is in progress and not all features are stable yet.</p>

<br>

<h2>🎯 Features</h2>

<h3>🔧 Modular Vehicle Assembly</h3>
<p>Craft frames, engines, wheels and attachments in the Auto Mechanic Table, then combine them on the Automobile Assembler. Mix and match parts to change speed, handling and looks.</p>

<h3>🛣️ Roads &amp; Tracks</h3>
<p>Build roads, racetracks and obstacle courses using slopes, dash panels and off-road tiles — shape the terrain your vehicles drive on.</p>

<h3>🧩 Data-Driven Parts</h3>
<p>Add custom vehicle types via data packs and resource packs — no code required to extend the vehicle roster.</p>

<h3>🅿️ Autopilot &amp; Signage</h3>
<p>Place Autopilot Signs to guide unmanned vehicles along a route.</p>

<h3>🎮 Full Driving Controls</h3>
<p>Accelerate, brake, steer, drift and burnout with a responsive control scheme built for Minecraft's movement feel.</p>

<br>

<tr><td><strong>Minecraft</strong></td><td>26.2</td></tr>

</table>

<br>

<h2>🎮 How to Use</h2>

<ol>
<li>Install the mod on <strong>both client and server</strong>.</li>
<li>Craft an Auto Mechanic Table and use it to craft frames, engines and wheels.</li>
<li>Craft an Automobile Assembler and a Crowbar, place your parts to build the vehicle, and drive.</li>
</ol>
